## Glossary

<!-- One line per term: name · one-sentence canonical definition · one-sentence boundary
     (what it is NOT / the concept it gets confused with). Alphabetical once there are a few. -->
- Account — one person's way into the app, identified by a unique email address and holding exactly one role: registered user or administrator. NOT a public identity (the email is never shown publicly; a public display name exists only after community opt-in), and NOT a personal goal or any of its tracking data.
- Account name — an optional, private name an account gives itself, used to greet it on Home and shown in its own Profile. NOT the public display name of community participation (that one is chosen separately, only on opt-in), and NOT visible to any other account, administrators included.
- Account time zone — the named time zone of one account, chosen in its profile (first taken from the browser at registration), that decides where its goals' days and weeks begin and end; a change applies to each goal from its next commitment or evaluation period. NOT a setting of one goal (the user never chooses a time zone per goal), and NOT the installation's default time zone, which only fills in when an account has none of its own.
- Acting user — the account, with its role, on whose behalf the application performs an action, or the anonymous visitor when nobody is signed in; it is established only by signing in. NOT the owner of the data being asked for (an administrator who asks about another account is still only the acting user, and gains no access by asking).
- Administrator — an account with the administrator role, which maintains the public catalog, reviews suggestions and feedback, and deactivates or reactivates accounts. NOT a super-user: the role never grants access to another person's private goals, activity, notes or personal reports.
- Anonymous visitor — someone using the app without being signed in, who can browse public pages only; a registered user who is signed out in a browser is an anonymous visitor there. NOT an account (the visitor's account, if they have one, still exists but plays no part until they sign in), and never the owner of any private data.
- Attempt — one continuous pursuit of a personal goal, from its start until it is ended; a new goal begins with attempt 1, and restarting an ended goal begins the next one with its own history and a streak from zero. NOT a pause (a pause interrupts an attempt without ending it), and NOT the goal itself (one goal can have several attempts over time).
- Catalog habit — a public, reusable description of a behavior (e.g. Reading, Yoga), maintained by administrators and visible to anyone once published. NOT a personal goal (one user's measurable commitment based on a habit), and NOT a signal that anyone's participation is public.
- Catalog habit state — where a catalog habit stands in curation: Draft (created, never published), Published (visible to everyone) or Unpublished (taken out of the public catalog after being published); only an administrator sees Draft and Unpublished habits. NOT an evaluation state (Pending, Met, Failed, Missed belong to commitments and evaluation periods), and NOT deletion (an unpublished habit still exists and can be published again).
- Category — one grouping from a fixed list (Body, Mind, Health, Money, Home, Other) that every catalog habit and custom habit belongs to, used to filter the catalog. NOT a tag a user invents, and NOT the habit's intent.
- Commitment — one scheduled day of a commitment-based goal, from midnight to midnight in the time zone in effect for that day, on which the user is expected to do the activity; it is evaluated on its own and can be Excused. NOT an evaluation period (a period-based goal has no individual commitments), and NOT a record (a commitment is what is expected; the record is what happened).
- Commitment-based goal — a personal goal on fixed days of the week, evaluated one commitment at a time. NOT a period-based goal ("3 times a week" is period-based even if the user usually trains on the same three days).
- Correction — a change of the records of a commitment or evaluation period whose result is already final (Met, Failed or Missed), evaluated with the goal settings version of its date exactly as if it had been recorded on time; it keeps no history of earlier values. NOT an edit lock being lifted (the 48-hour grace period never locks records), and NOT a change of the goal's settings (a correction changes what happened, never the target).
- Custom habit — a behavior created privately by one registered user for their own goals, visible only to its owner; administrators have none. NOT a catalog habit (never public, never in community statistics, not visible to administrators).
- Demo account — a synthetic registered-user account in the reserved `example.com` domain (`demo1@example.com` to `demo3@example.com`, created by demo loading, and `demo4@example.com`, created by demo history loading), created at start with the operator's demo password, so that reviewers can sign in without registering and later slices have users to attach their demo data to; its private demo data (such as custom habits, goals, records and notes) is created only together with the account. NOT a real person's account (it carries no real name or address; demo1's account name "Demo One" is a label, not a person's name), and NOT an administrator (the demo data has no administrator of its own).
- Demo history loading — the startup task, run after demo loading, that creates `demo4@example.com` together with goals that started 28 days earlier and their records and notes, relative to the date of the run, so that the demo shows Met, Failed, Missed, Pending and Due results; an account that already exists is left unchanged. NOT demo loading (whose results never name `demo4@example.com`), and NOT a re-basing of existing data (a database keeps the history of the day it was first loaded until a demo refresh).
- Demo refresh — bringing the hosted demo back to the state of a freshly deployed empty database, on which administrator creation, demo loading and demo history loading run again; it is part of every deployment, the operator can also start one on its own, without a new commit, and the hosting schedule starts one once a day until 2027-01-31. NOT demo loading itself (demo loading runs at every start and never resets anything), and NOT a correction or deletion of a user's data elsewhere.
- Deployment — one rollout of a chosen commit of the app to an installation, such as the hosted demo, started by the operator and ending as succeeded or failed. NOT the installation itself (the hosted demo or a local copy), and NOT a merge to `master` (a merge alone deploys nothing).
- Due — the evaluation state of a commitment or evaluation period that has begun and not yet ended, during which it can be recorded as it happens. NOT overdue (an ended commitment is Pending, not late), and NOT Upcoming (which has not begun).
- Evaluation calendar — the commitments or evaluation periods of one goal over a range of dates, each with the instants at which it starts and ends. NOT a report (it carries no result such as Met or Missed), and NOT the user's records.
- Evaluation state — exactly one of Upcoming, Due, Pending, Met, Failed or Missed for one commitment or evaluation period at a given time, always derived from its records, the goal settings version of its date and that time, never entered by the user and never stored; Met, Failed and Missed are final. NOT a catalog habit state, and NOT Excused or a pause (those exempt a commitment or a goal from evaluation).
- Evaluation period — one day, or one Monday-to-Sunday week, over which a period-based goal is assessed as a whole; a weekly goal's first period is the first full week that starts on or after its start date. NOT a commitment (a period cannot be Excused, only paused), and NOT a rolling window (periods are fixed calendar days or weeks).
- Excused — the user-chosen state of an individual scheduled commitment in a commitment-based goal that exempts it from evaluation, requiring no approval and optionally carrying a reason, and that can be set only while the commitment is upcoming or Pending, never after it has been finalized as Missed or Failed; it is excluded from consistency and preserves but does not extend the streak. NOT Pause (a pause suspends a whole goal over a date interval and applies to period-based goals too; excused covers one commitment of a commitment-based goal), and NOT Pending (pending is temporary and resolves on its own; excused is the user's deliberate, lasting choice).
- Failed — the finalized outcome of a commitment or evaluation period whose recorded activity did not meet the applicable target: below the required amount for a Build goal, or above the permitted amount for a Reduce goal. NOT Missed (a missed outcome has no record at all), and NOT partial progress (the recorded amount is preserved and reported; Failed is only the evaluation verdict on it).
- Goal settings version — the settings of a personal goal (Build or Reduce, measurement, target, schedule and time zone) together with the date from which they apply; a change adds a version instead of overwriting, so every past commitment or period is judged by the version in effect for its date. NOT the goal (the goal keeps its identity, name and history across versions), and NOT an attempt (a new version continues the same attempt).
- Grace period — the system-wide 48 elapsed hours after a commitment or evaluation period ends, during which it is Pending and can still be recorded on time; a clock change in between does not change its length, so its end can read 23:00 or 01:00 on the local clock. NOT an edit lock (records can be corrected after it), and NOT a calendar interval of two local days.
- Hosted demo — the one public, Azure-hosted installation of the app that reviewers open in a browser, with its own database, secret configuration and operator log. NOT a local or test installation, and NOT a deployment (a deployment is one rollout of a commit to it).
- Intent — Build or Reduce as the direction a catalog habit or custom habit is usually pursued in, shown with the habit and used as a catalog filter. NOT a personal goal's Build or Reduce, which the goal itself defines (SPEC §5.1).
- Met — the finalized outcome of a commitment or evaluation period whose records meet the target of the goal settings version of its date: at or above the required amount for a Build goal, at or below the permitted amount for a Reduce goal (shown as "Met" for both). NOT Pending with enough progress (a result is Met only once its grace period has ended), and NOT a Reduce day with nothing recorded (that is Missed).
- Missed — the finalized outcome of a commitment or evaluation period that has no record once its 48-hour grace period has ended, judged from the records as they are now, for Build and Reduce goals alike; a later record turns it into Met or Failed, and deleting the only record of a final commitment makes it Missed. It counts as unsuccessful in consistency and resets the streak. NOT Failed (a failure has a record that fell short of the target), and NOT Pending (pending is still inside the grace period and not yet final).
- Named time zone — a place-based time zone of the standard time zone database, such as Europe/Kyiv or UTC, whose clock changes follow that place's rules. NOT a fixed offset such as +02:00 (it would miss clock changes), and NOT an abbreviation such as EET.
- Note — an optional private text of at most 500 characters on one commitment or evaluation period (not on a single record), written at any time after its day has begun, final results included, to give context such as why a result was Missed or partial. NOT part of the evaluation (a note never changes the state), and NOT visible to anyone but the goal's owner.
- Operator — the person who runs an installation of the app (such as the hosted demo), starts its deployments and demo refreshes (the hosted demo also refreshes itself once a day on a schedule), holds its secret configuration (administrator credentials, demo password), and reads its operator log. NOT an administrator (an operator is not an account and has no role in the app), and NOT one of the three audiences.
- Pending — the not-yet-final state of a commitment or evaluation period that has ended but whose 48-hour grace period is still open, whether or not any activity has been recorded so far; it is excluded from consistency and never resets a streak. NOT Missed (missed is final once the grace period has elapsed with no record), and NOT Excused (excused is a deliberate, lasting exemption by the user, while pending is temporary and resolves on its own).
- Period-based goal — a personal goal of N times per day or per week, evaluated one evaluation period at a time by counting what was recorded in it. NOT a commitment-based goal (no individual day is expected, so none can be Excused).
- Personal goal — one registered user's measurable commitment to Build or Reduce a behavior, based on one catalog habit or custom habit, with its own target, schedule, start date and optional name, private to its owner; one habit can support several independent goals. NOT the habit it is based on (editing or unpublishing the habit never changes the goal's settings), and NOT a suggested starting goal (a suggestion is only an example to start from).
- Record — what actually happened on one date for one personal goal: "Yes" or "No", a whole number of the goal's unit, or whole minutes, kept as entered below, at or above the target; it belongs to the commitment or evaluation period of its date, not to the instant it was saved, and a commitment has at most one. NOT the result (the evaluation state is derived from records), and NOT a note.
- Registered user — an account with the registered-user role, which owns its private goals, activity, notes, custom habits and personal reports. NOT an administrator (registration never grants that role), and NOT a community participant (participation is off until the user opts in).
- Seed catalog — the catalog habits that demo loading creates when the catalog is empty, so that a new installation shows a full catalog after its first deployment. NOT a protected or system-owned part of the catalog (administrators edit and unpublish seed habits like any other), and NOT demo users' private data.
- Sign-in block — a temporary state of an account, after 5 failed sign-ins in a row, in which sign-in fails for 5 minutes even with the correct password. NOT deactivation (an administrator's lasting decision that ends all access), and never shown as such to the person signing in.
- Suggested starting goal — an optional, fully customizable example target shown with a catalog habit (up to five per habit, one of them shown on the catalog card), described by the same fields a goal starts from: Build or Reduce, measurement, amount and unit, and schedule. NOT a prescribed target or coaching advice, and NOT a personal goal until a user creates one from it.
- Target text — the short words that state a goal's or a suggested starting goal's target and schedule, such as "15 min, 5 days a week" or "at most 1 sweet a day"; a goal without a name of its own is named by its habit's current name and this text. A Reduce yes/no goal on fixed days reads "avoided on …" instead, since each such day is met when the behavior is not done; a suggestion keeps its catalog text. NOT the goal's name when it has one of its own, and NOT the evaluation rule (the text describes the target; the settings version decides how it is evaluated).
- Upcoming — the evaluation state of a commitment or evaluation period that has not begun yet; nothing can be recorded for it. NOT Due (which has begun), and NOT a reminder (the app sends none).
