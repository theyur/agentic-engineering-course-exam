# KeyValue

A two-column list of labels and values for read-only settings: `dl.ht-kv` with `dt` (muted) and `dd` (semibold).

- Labels are nouns ("Schedule"), values are short ("Mon · Wed · Fri").
- For editable settings link to the form ("Edit settings") instead of inline editing.
- Used by the goal page's Settings card and the New goal summary card (slice 4). A goal's own Build or Reduce is the row "Build or reduce", never "Intent" (`CONTEXT.md`).
