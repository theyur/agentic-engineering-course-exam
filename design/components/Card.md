# Card

A card is the main container on every page: white `surface`, 1px `line` border, `radius-md`, `shadow-card`, 18px padding.

- Optional parts: `ht-eyebrow` (uppercase label), `ht-card__title` (`heading-3`), `ht-card__sub` (small muted line).
- One topic per card. Do not nest cards; inside a card use `surface-2` boxes (see GoalStats) or rows.
- No coloured left borders or coloured card backgrounds.
