# BandSummary

Four Consistency Bands with participant counts, used as the community preview on the visitor Home.

- Bands and limits are fixed: 90%+, 70%+, 50%+, Below 50% (rolling 30 days, at least 10 finalized evaluations).
- Always add the sentence: "people who opted in · names listed alphabetically, never ranked."
- Never show a rank number, a podium, "top", "best" or "leaderboard".
