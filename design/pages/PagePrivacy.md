# PagePrivacy

Privacy: what stays private and who can see what. Anyone can open it, from the "Privacy" link in the Footer.

- Page head `heading-1` "Privacy", one lead sentence, then three Cards in one column (at most 720px wide):
  - "What stays private": goals, records, notes and custom habits are private;
  - "Community comparison is opt-in": off by default, can be turned off at any time, leaving removes the person from the community views and keeps their private history, custom habits never appear in community statistics;
  - "What administrators can see": administrators manage the catalog and accounts and cannot see goals, records, notes or personal reports.
- States only promises SPEC makes (§2, §3, §7.1, §8). No legal text, no cookie banner, no dates.
- Same TopNav and Footer as every page; the visitor or signed-in variant follows the session.
