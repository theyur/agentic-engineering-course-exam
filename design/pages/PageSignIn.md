# PageSignIn

Sign in: a centered 420px column (`ht-auth`) with one Card form.

- Email, Password, "Keep me signed in", "Forgot password?", full-width primary "Sign in"; below it "New here? Create an account".
- Errors: one message under the password field, never which of the two was wrong. Keep the email value.
- A deactivated account sees a Notice: "This account is deactivated. Contact the administrators."
- Built on ASP.NET Core Identity pages, restyled with these classes.
