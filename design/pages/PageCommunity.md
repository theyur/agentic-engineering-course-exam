# PageCommunity

The full Community page: participation status, Consistency Bands with names, rules, and catalog habit performance.

- Notice at top: signed-in participant sees their public name and band + "Change in Profile"; non-participants see "Take part" (goes to Profile → Community); visitors see "Register and opt in to appear here".
- Left: BandList (alphabetical names, "+ N more"). Right: "How bands work" (30 days, 10 results minimum, own commitments, no ranks).
- Below: Catalog habit performance DataTable — band and contributor count, or "Insufficient community data" with fewer than 5 contributors (never the value).
