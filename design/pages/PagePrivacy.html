Privacy

Habit Tracker is a personal tracker. What you track is yours.

What stays private

Your goals, records, notes and custom habits are private. Only you can see them.

Community comparison is opt-in

Community comparison is off by default. You take part only if you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time. When you stop, you are removed from the community views; your private history stays. Custom habits never appear in community statistics.

What administrators can see

Administrators manage the public catalog and accounts. They cannot see your goals, records, notes or personal reports.
