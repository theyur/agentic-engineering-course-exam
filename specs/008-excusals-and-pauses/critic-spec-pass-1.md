# Critic pass 1: spec for 008-excusals-and-pauses

**Verdict**: REVISE
**Date**: 2026-11-21
**Reviewed**: specs/008-excusals-and-pauses/spec.md, specs/008-excusals-and-pauses/checklists/requirements.md (cross-checked against CONTEXT.md and docs/roadmap.md as changed in this change, and docs/adr/0023-demo-pause-loading.md)

## Notes

### MAJOR-1: D-1 (SPEC §11.2) rejects real alternatives and spans features, but has no ADR
- **Where**: spec.md "Decisions due before acceptance criteria", D-1 (l. 691-694); Clarifications, Session 2026-11-21 (specify), first Q (l. 24-26); Assumptions (l. 712-713: "A later community slice computes its figures with the exemptions of FR-013 and FR-014"); docs/roadmap.md SPEC §11.2 row (links only the spec).
- **Against**: constitution, Decision Discipline: "A decision that spans features, is hard to reverse, or rejects real alternatives MUST also become an ADR in `docs/adr/`." The project's own reading of that rule: slice 5 spec l. 813 ("plus an ADR where it spans features") and slice 6 spec l. 895-898 (D-4 "spans features (community, slice 9, computes from the same figures), so it is recorded in ADR 0020"). Precedent: specs/006-note-editing/critic-spec-pass-1.md MAJOR on a missing ADR under the same clause.
- **Reasoning**: D-1 decides which weekly evaluations count at all: a week with one paused date leaves consistency and streaks as a whole (FR-007). The spec names two rejected alternatives (Paused only when all 7 days are paused; a target scaled to the unpaused days) and an accepted cost. So it meets the "rejects real alternatives" trigger. The spec's own Assumptions say slice 9 computes Consistency Bands and Catalog Habit Performance with these exemptions, so D-1 also decides which evaluations count as "eligible finalized evaluations" in SPEC §7.2/§7.3. That meets the "spans features" trigger, which is how slice 6 decided that D-4 needed ADR 0020. D-1 is recorded only in the spec's Clarifications and the roadmap row. No ADR exists: `docs/adr/` ends at 0023, and 0023 covers only D-4. A later community slice that wants a different partial-week rule would be changing a recorded decision. Decision Discipline requires a new or superseding ADR for that, which needs an ADR to supersede in the first place. The same clause arguably also covers D-2, which narrows SPEC §5.3's "Users may mark individual scheduled commitments as Excused" to Due or Pending only and rejects two named alternatives. The spec doesn't say why neither decision gets an ADR. As written, `/speckit-plan`'s Constitution Check can't mark Decision Discipline as met.

### MINOR-1: US3-AS4's trend leaves out the unrecorded 17 and 18 November
- **Where**: spec.md US3-AS4 (l. 273-275); the scenario's data (l. 259-263: "nothing recorded from 17 November on").
- **Against**: slice 6 FR-006 ("A bar whose commitment, day or week has no record has no value"); locked F007 AC-023 (`personal-reports.feature`), which lists every bar, e.g. "(2026-11-17, no value)".
- **Reasoning**: The 14 bars run from 6 to 19 November. With no records from 17 November on, the bars for 17, 18 and 19 all have no value and no mark. The scenario names only "11 and 19 November" as having no value and no mark. Every other bar is listed by kind (Excused, Paused), so the reader takes the list as exhaustive and infers that 17 and 18 have a value. The F007 convention lists every bar, so a translator following US3-AS4 as worded either gets an inconsistent criterion or has to fix the example. The checklist says the date arithmetic of every worked example was checked (requirements.md l. 47-48), and this one slipped through.

### MINOR-2: The order of the excused and paused record refusals is not fixed
- **Where**: spec.md FR-005 (l. 436-438), FR-009 (l. 457-459); Edge Cases, first bullet (l. 372-374).
- **Against**: slice 5 FR-005 (l. 503-512), which numbers every step (4 date, 5 value, 6 note, 7 limit), and slice 5 FR-018 ("MUST check in the order of FR-005").
- **Reasoning**: Both new refusals are "checked after slice 5 FR-005 step (4)". The spec doesn't say whether they come before or after step (5) (value), (6) (note) and (7) (50 records), so an invalid value on a paused date has two possible results. It also doesn't fix the order between the two. A commitment can be both excused and paused: a Due commitment excused today, then paused from today (edge case l. 372-374). Recording it could give either "This commitment is excused. Remove the excusal to record it." or "This goal is paused on this date.". FR-007 gives the pause precedence for the state, but no FR gives the precedence for the refusal. Earlier slices fix every step, so the acceptance criteria and the implementation would each have to guess.

### MINOR-3: An upcoming pause can pause the current week while the goal counts as not paused
- **Where**: spec.md FR-007 (l. 450-453), FR-018 (l. 489-490), FR-019 (l. 491-494), FR-010 (l. 462-466), US2-AS12 (l. 232-237), HP-001 (l. 554-560), HP-004 (l. 577-580); Edge Cases (l. 377-381).
- **Against**: FR-012's "upcoming" (first day later than the current date) and FR-011 ("Only a pause that has not begun can be cancelled"); CONTEXT.md Pause (l. 64).
- **Reasoning**: Under FR-007 (D-1), a per-week period is Paused while any of its dates is in a pause, upcoming pauses included. A pause set on Monday for Thursday onward therefore pauses the current week at once. US2-AS12 relies on this: on Wednesday 18 November the pause from the 19th has not begun, yet recording gives "This week is paused." FR-018 and FR-019 still treat that goal as not paused, because no current pause covers its current date. So it stays on the Active tab and counts as an active goal. It has no Due entry and can't be recorded. The goal page shows "Pause" but no paused Notice, and "Resume" gives "This goal is not paused." The same state follows a mid-week resume (edge case l. 377-379). No FR, edge case or page requirement describes what the user sees in this state or confirms that it is intended. The Edge Cases only say that one paused day exempts the whole week. A paused current week on a goal reported as not paused is easy to build two ways.

### MINOR-4: Several earlier reads that return a state don't say what an Excused or Paused entry gives
- **Where**: spec.md FR-015 (l. 480-481, "no progress verdict"), FR-017 (l. 486-488), "Earlier requirements and tests this slice changes" (l. 629-630, "Slice 5 FR-002's and FR-009's (history) results gain the exemptions"); Edge Cases, "Notes" (l. 393-394); FR-009 (l. 459-461).
- **Against**: slice 5 FR-006 (recording, changing and deleting return the state after the change and whether it was a correction); slice 5 FR-008 (recent notes each carry "the state at the supplied time"); slice 5 FR-009 (history gives progress and "whether a change now would be a correction"); slice 6 FR-005 (recent results carry progress); slice 6 FR-012 (dashboard recent notes carry "the state").
- **Reasoning**: (a) "No progress verdict" (FR-015) is not a defined term. Slice 6 FR-005 gives progress, not a verdict, so the spec doesn't say whether an exempt recent result keeps its progress. FR-017 likewise lists records and note but not progress. (b) FR-009 lets a record on a Paused date be changed or deleted. The spec doesn't say what slice 5 FR-006's result then returns: "Paused", or the state it would have without the pause? It also doesn't say whether such a change is a correction, or what history's correction flag is for an exempt row. (c) Notes may sit on Excused or Paused entries (edge case l. 393-394), but slice 5 FR-008 and the dashboard's recent notes (slice 6 FR-012) aren't extended to say which state such a note shows. (d) The cross-reference to "Slice 5 FR-002" is wrong. Slice 5 FR-002 is the date-acceptance rule and returns no result. The requirement that returns a state is FR-006, and FR-008 is missing from the list.

### MINOR-5: HP-001 says a Paused row offers no record action, but FR-009 lets its record be changed or deleted
- **Where**: spec.md HP-001, History bullet (l. 561-563: "a Paused row offers no record action"); FR-009 (l. 459-461); US2-AS10 (l. 227-229: "it can be changed or deleted, and the day stays Paused").
- **Against**: design/pages/PageGoalDetail.html History table (rows with a record have the ghost action "Edit"), slice 5's record actions.
- **Reasoning**: A record kept on a Paused date (US2-AS10) can be changed or deleted at the application level. On the page, though, the Paused row "offers no record action", and the History row is where "Edit" lives in the design. Either the page has no way to do what FR-009 allows, or "no record action" means only "no Record button" and "Edit" stays. Either way, page implementers and UI tests can't tell which.

### MINOR-6: FR-016 doesn't say whether a Paused commitment's records count in its week's value
- **Where**: spec.md FR-016 (l. 482-485).
- **Against**: slice 6 FR-006 ("The value counts the records of every state, Due and Pending included"); FR-009 (records stored before the pause are kept, l. 459-460); US2-AS10.
- **Reasoning**: For a commitment-based yes/no goal, FR-016 takes Excused and Paused commitments out of the week's Build target but says nothing about its value. A commitment can keep a "Yes" record after a pause starts on its date (US2-AS10, edge case l. 375-376). Under slice 6's rule, that record still counts in the week's value. The bar can then show more done than the target, for example 3 against 2. The Reduce target of 0 is affected the same way. The spec states no rule, so the value can be computed two ways.

### MINOR-7: FR-031 and ADR 0023 store data "as if made at its own time", but FR-025 stores no time
- **Where**: spec.md FR-031 (l. 525-527: "stored as if each had been made at its own time"); FR-025 (l. 513-514: "stored as facts with their dates and reason ... nothing else is stored"); FR-003 (l. 428: "from the moment it is excused"); docs/adr/0023-demo-pause-loading.md, Decision ("Excusals and pauses are stored as if each had been made at its own time").
- **Against**: ADR 0018 and ADR 0020 (states derived on read from stored facts and the supplied time).
- **Reasoning**: FR-025 stores only dates and reasons, so an excusal or a pause has no time of its own. "As if made at its own time" then has no meaning that storage or a test could check. This phrase appears in neither slice 5's nor slice 6's demo requirements. FR-003's "from the moment it is excused" suggests the state depends on when the excusal was made, and FR-025 rules that out. A read whose supplied time is earlier than the excusal call would still show Excused. FR-008 relies on the rule for when a pause can start, not on any stored time. Planning could reasonably read this as a call for a timestamp column, which FR-025 forbids.

### MINOR-8: The glossary has no "Excusal" entry, and its "Evaluation state" still says "exactly one of"
- **Where**: spec.md FR terms (l. 404-405, *Excusal*; l. 409-411, an Excused or Paused one "has no Upcoming, Due, Pending, Met, Failed or Missed state"), Key Entities (l. 654-655), HP-012 (l. 607-612); CONTEXT.md l. 51 (Evaluation state, unchanged), l. 64 (Pause: "NOT an excusal").
- **Against**: constitution I ("A change that introduces a new domain term, or sharpens the meaning of an existing one, MUST add or update its `CONTEXT.md` entry ... in the same change").
- **Reasoning**: The spec introduces "excusal" as a domain noun: a key entity, the UI action "Remove excusal", and the texts "This excusal can no longer be removed." and "This commitment is not excused.". The new Pause entry uses it as if it were defined ("NOT an excusal"). Pause got both a noun and an adjective entry, but excusal has only the adjective "Excused". Separately, CONTEXT.md still says each commitment or period has "exactly one of Upcoming, Due, Pending, Met, Failed or Missed ... at a given time". The spec now rules that an Excused or Paused one has none of them. The glossary's Evaluation state entry should have been sharpened in this change, and it wasn't.

### MINOR-10: Several acceptance scenarios leave out data a translator needs
- **Where**: spec.md US1 header (l. 146-147: measurement not given, while US1-AS10 records "No", which implies yes/no); US2-AS11 (l. 230-231: days and measurement of the commitment-based goal); US2-AS12 (l. 232: start date, measurement and the time the pause is set for "Yoga, 3 times a week"); US3 data (l. 262: "paused on 13 November at 08:00" gives no last day, though FR-006 requires one); US3-AS6 and US3-AS7 (l. 279-282: no goal, dates or records); US4-AS1 to AS4 (l. 301-308: the goals not defined).
- **Against**: the earlier slices' convention of fully specified scenarios with supplied times, e.g. F007 AC-022 to AC-025; spec.md "Test data" (l. 123-125).
- **Reasoning**: These scenarios state results without the inputs that produce them. For US3's pause, the last day of 15 November can only be inferred from "13 to 15 November no record". US3-AS6 ("every final result is Excused or Paused") also clashes with the spec's own terms: by l. 409-413, an Excused or Paused result is never a final result, so a goal with no final result at all would satisfy it. The translator has to invent the data, which shifts product decisions into `/speckit-ac-translate`.

## Summary
1 MAJOR, 10 MINOR. The spec covers SPEC §5.3 and the excused and paused parts of §6. I checked the worked figures against slice 5's 48-hour rule and slice 6's consistency and streak rules: US3's "90% (10 of 11)", the streaks of 2 and 8, the 17 recent results and the 83% variant; FR-031's "95% (22 of 23)", 10/12, "94% (18 of 19)", 2/16 and the dashboard's 1 Due, 2 Pending and 0 Missed on every weekday. The only arithmetic slip is the trend omission in US3-AS4. The catalog habits "Meditation" and "Drinking water" are Published in the seed catalog, and the target texts follow slice 4's rule. The design classes named in HP-006 exist. The test impact on the UI, host and smoke-check tests is acknowledged. The blocking issue is Decision Discipline: D-1 rejects named alternatives and, by the spec's own Assumptions, feeds the community slice, so it needs an ADR, as slice 6's D-4 got ADR 0020. The minor notes are gaps that `/speckit-ac-translate` and `/speckit-plan` would otherwise have to fill by guessing. Most are about check order (MINOR-2), what states and progress exempt entries return (MINOR-4 to MINOR-6) and the per-week Paused state of a goal that counts as not paused (MINOR-3).
