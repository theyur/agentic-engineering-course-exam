## Notes

### MAJOR-1: The D-5 decision (a daily scheduled refresh) rests on a premise that the spec's own evaluation rules contradict
- **Where**: ADR 0018 "Context" (l. 14-16: "demo data written on one day shows Pending and Due only for about two days"), "Options" D-5 option 2 (l. 47-48: "Between refreshes nothing is Pending after two days, so the demo would show its states only on the day of a deployment or refresh. Rejected."), and option 2 for D-4 (l. 33-34: "the hosted app would have to stay always on"); spec.md Clarifications, critic-revision Q2 (l. 60-62), whose question already assumes the states would stop showing; HP-009 (l. 616-620: "Data written on day R still shows Pending and Due on day R + 1, so one late or failed scheduled run is tolerated"); D-5 (l. 722-724).
- **Against**: spec.md FR-010 (l. 476: Pending is "ended and its grace period ... has not ended, with or without records"), FR-012 (l. 489-492), US5-AS1 (every commitment in the range is returned, recorded or not), HP-001 (History lists every commitment that has begun); FR-030 (the Reading goal runs on all seven days and has no end); SC-005 (l. 701-702); ADR 0015 l. 31 and l. 63 (App Service B1 is already "Always On").
- **Continues**: new
- **Reasoning**: Under D-2 (FR-010, FR-012), every ended commitment is Pending for 48 hours whether or not it has a record. "Reading, 20 min daily" has a commitment on every date and no end date. So on any day D after the run, D is Due and D − 1 and D − 2 are Pending (unrecorded). The Met, Failed (12 minutes, 3 sweets) and Missed results written by demo history loading are final and never change. Yoga's current week is always Due. Without any refresh, demo4 therefore shows Met, Failed, Missed, Pending and Due, a partial amount and a note on every day until 2027-01-31, which is everything SC-005 asks for. The ADR's only stated reason for rejecting "manual refreshes only" is false, and so is HP-009's implied claim that the data stops working after day R + 1. The owner's D-5 answer was given to a question that assumed this false premise (Clarifications l. 60-61), and the ADR records it as Accepted. The chosen option is not free. Every day, all reviewer accounts and changes are deleted and all sessions end (slice 3 HP-018), and the hosted demo is down for about 10 minutes (ADR 0018 l. 78-79). A second false drawback weakens the D-4 comparison as well: the hosted app is already Always On under ADR 0015. Constitution, Decision Discipline, requires the owner's answer and an ADR for a decision that rejects real alternatives. A record that rejects an alternative on a false ground does not meet that requirement, and the slice's hosting behaviour (HP-009, and the HP-007 and HP-008 plan work) is built on it. Without a refresh, only these things change: the History's first rows fill with Missed, and Pending results that already carry a record (R − 2) last only one day. The ADR never weighs either point.

### MINOR-2: The daily schedule outlives the hosted demo's teardown, contrary to ADR 0018
- **Where**: ADR 0018 "Decision" (l. 67-68: "The schedule is removed with the resource group after 2027-01-31."); spec.md HP-009 (l. 617-619).
- **Against**: `.github/workflows/deploy-demo.yml` (the trigger lives in the repository file, and the job uses `environment: demo`, l. 49); `tools/hosting/teardown.ps1` l. 1-3 and l. 63-70 (it deletes the resource group, the app registration and the GitHub environment `demo`, and never touches the workflow file); GitHub Actions behaviour (a job that names an environment that does not exist creates it).
- **Continues**: new
- **Reasoning**: Deleting the resource group does not remove a `schedule:` trigger in a workflow on `master`. After teardown, the daily run would keep starting. It would recreate an empty `demo` environment and fail at the Azure sign-in every day. No requirement in the spec and no step in the ADR removes or disables the trigger, and the claim in the ADR is false.

### MINOR-6: The time zone of the run date R is not stated, and ADR 0018 assumes the hosted default is UTC
- **Where**: spec.md FR-030 (l. 539-542: "uses the installation's default time zone ... relative to the run date R"); ADR 0018 l. 67 ("The installation's default time zone is UTC, so `demo4`'s days match the refresh"); HP-009 (l. 618: "shortly after midnight UTC").
- **Against**: slice 4 FR-025 and spec l. 815-816 and l. 1137 (the default is an operator setting); ADR 0016 l. 44-45 and l. 79 (the hosted demo's operator sets it); `src/HT.Ui/Operator/StartupTasks.cs` l. 26 (`Operator:DefaultTimeZone`).
- **Continues**: new
- **Reasoning**: FR-030 never says that R is the date in demo4's time zone at the instant of the run. If the default zone is behind UTC, for example America/New_York at a run at 00:10 UTC, the UTC date is a day that has not begun for the account. Then "Due (R)" and "every record is on a date before R" no longer hold, and FR-002 would refuse such records for a user. The schedule's alignment with demo4's days depends on the hosted setting staying unset, and neither the spec nor the ADR states that as a requirement.

## Summary
1 MAJOR and 6 MINOR. All 14 notes of pass 1 are resolved, and none has regressed. The demo4 design no longer touches the locked F001 and F004 criteria. The Yoga data is bounded and checks out for every weekday. The note rule and the correction path through the History are now well defined. The evaluation core (FR-001 to FR-018) is consistent and could go to `/speckit-ac-translate`. The MAJOR note is about the D-5 decision. Its ADR and the owner question rest on the claim that demo data stops showing Pending and Due after two days. Under the spec's own FR-010 and FR-012, that claim is false. So the daily wipe of the hosted demo, with its reviewer-facing costs, was chosen against a mis-stated alternative and should be put to the owner again with correct facts. The minor notes cover the knock-on effects on slice 3's shipped texts and glossary entries, a schedule that survives teardown, host tests that cannot stay unchanged, the glossary update that is still not done, design texts missing from the change list, and the time zone of the run date.
